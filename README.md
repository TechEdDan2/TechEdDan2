<summary><h3> 🚶 More About Me and My Path </h3></summary>
With over 15 years in education, I have achieved a number of personal and professional goals, including the successful creation and leadership of a comprehensive Technology Education and Computer Science Department. I began my career in Library Services and IT Support, which provided a foundational understanding of institutional technology needs. Pivoting to the classroom, I leveraged my Masters of Information Science and independent learning to design and implement a robust Computer Science curriculum for High School and Middle School students. The curriculum, spanning courses like AP Computer Science A, Website Design, IB Digital Society, iOS App Development, Computer Science Principles, LEGO Robotics, and Digital Literacy and Citizenship, required me to continually master various coding languages, CAD, multimedia editing, networking, ed tech tools, and hardware platforms. Furthermore, I co-founded and continue to coach a successful FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) Robotics program, which directly involves hardware/software control systems development. Now, I am channeling my passion for technology and technical problem-solving into looking for new challenges, including diving more deeply into software development to create, not just teach. To formalize this transition and accelerate my shift from teaching technical concepts to creating professional-grade software, I am completing Stony Brook University's intensive Software Engineering Certificate Bootcamp. The challenge of balancing a full-time teaching position with an intensive technical program only reinforces my dedication to this new path.
